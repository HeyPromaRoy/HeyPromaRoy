## Selected research

Presented orally and as a poster at LM4UC @ IJCAI 2026. Introduces the Tokenization Equity Audit benchmark for measuring unequal token cost across languages.
- **[Structural Silence: When AI Infrastructure Fails Speakers of Underrepresented Languages](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.12278)** — with [Avijit Roy](https://avijitroy.com/research/). Presented as a poster at the 69th Annual International Linguistic Association Conference. Examines web representation, training data, tokenization, and connectivity barriers through Bengali.
